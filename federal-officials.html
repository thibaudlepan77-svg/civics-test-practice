Eight answers are missing from the booklet, not four

Four of them are the ones above, and they are the same for everyone in the country. The other four depend on where you live, your two senators, your representative, your governor and your state capital. Pick your state and those are filled in too.

Together that is eight questions out of the 128 that you cannot revise from the official document. Five of the eight carry a star, which means they are in the 20 question set used for the 65/20 rule. If you are 68 with 20 years of residence, a quarter of your entire test is blank on the official sheet.
