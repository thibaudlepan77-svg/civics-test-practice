Privacy policy

This policy covers the Android app Magyar Interjú, published on Google Play under the developer name Jaayleer.

The app collects no personal data. It has no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It does not use the microphone, so nothing you say out loud is recorded or sent anywhere.

Your language choice and whether you bought the full kit are kept on your phone only. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Payment for the full kit goes through Google Play. Google handles it under its own privacy policy, and we never see your card or bank details. We only receive the confirmation that the purchase was made, without your name.

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Questions, or a request about your data, write to thibaudlepanpro@gmail.com.
