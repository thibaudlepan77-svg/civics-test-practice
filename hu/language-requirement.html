The Hungarian language requirement, what the consul actually checks

Is there a language test?

Not a written one. For simplified naturalization the consulates say your Hungarian does not have to be proven with a language exam. No certificate, no score. The officer who takes your application talks with you in Hungarian and records on the form whether you can communicate.

What level is expected

The Tel Aviv consulate describes it as intermediate level communication skills in Hungarian, which includes both comprehension and verbal expression. The conversation has to work both ways, you understand the question and you answer it. Dialect, non-standard Hungarian or broken Hungarian is not a problem in itself.

The information letter the consulates publish is more concrete. You must be able to hand in your application on your own, without help, and answer the officer's questions on your own, in short sentences. If your Hungarian does not reach that level, they write, you cannot expect a positive decision.

Who is checked

Every applicant aged 14 or more, and those who turn 14 in the six months after the application. It is the same for the ancestry route and for the marriage route, both require the language. There is no statutory exemption. The only adjustment is for someone who has lost their hearing or speech, with a medical certificate, and then the check is done in writing.

It can be checked again later

The consulates warn that your Hungarian can be examined at any stage, for example when you later apply for a passport, or by phone if the authorities in Hungary want to verify something in your file.

Writing counts too

The application form exists only in Hungarian. Every applicant over 14 writes a short CV by hand, in Hungarian, inside the form. You prepare it in advance, but it has to be in your own handwriting.

What the conversation is about

The consulates do not publish a list of questions. Applicants and teachers report the same topics again and again. Who you are, where you were born and live, your family, your Hungarian ancestor and their dates and places, how you learned Hungarian, and why you want citizenship. Treat this as what people report, not as an official list.

Not to be confused with

The constitutional exam with written topics is for ordinary naturalization, people who have lived in Hungary for years. The cultural knowledge exam introduced on 1 January 2025 is for national and EU residence cards, according to the immigration office. Neither is part of simplified naturalization.
