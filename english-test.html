The English test, reading and writing

The civics questions are not the whole test. You also have to read one sentence out loud and write one down. Most people spend every hour on the 128 questions and none here, which is a mistake, because this part is short and completely predictable.

PartWhat happensTo pass
ReadingThe officer shows you up to 3 sentencesRead 1 correctly, out loud
WritingThe officer reads up to 3 sentences to youWrite 1 correctly
SpeakingNo separate testJudged from your normal answers during the interview

Why this part is easier than it looks

USCIS publishes the complete vocabulary list used to build those sentences. Every sentence you can be shown is made only of words from these lists. Learn the lists and there are no surprises left. Both documents carry the revision mark 08/08, read on 2026-08-24.
