The rest of the test

These four are the only answers that change with your state. Everything else is the same wherever you live. All 128 questions of the 2025 test, the 100 questions of the 2008 test, and which of the two is yours. There is a free mock test on both pages.

Check these four the week of your interview and not months before. Senators and governors change with elections, and the officer wants the person serving on the day you are interviewed.
