# Free practice for the 2025 USCIS civics test

All 128 official questions with every accepted answer, a mock test that follows
the real stopping rule, and the four answers that depend on where you live,
filled in for all 50 states and the District of Columbia.

Since October 20, 2025 the officer draws from 128 questions and asks up to 20.
You need 12 correct, and the officer stops the moment you reach 12, or 9 wrong.
Applicants who are 65 or older with 20 years as a permanent resident take the
65/20 test instead, 10 questions drawn from the 20 starred ones, 6 correct to
pass, answered in the language of your choice.

## The part that is not in the official booklet

Questions 23, 29, 61 and 62 are answered `Answers will vary` in the USCIS
material, because they depend on your state. This site answers them, per state,
from congress.gov, house.gov and usa.gov read on 2026-08-24.

## Sources

The questions and their accepted answers are official USCIS material, a work of
the United States government. Federal office holders change, so check
[uscis.gov test updates](https://www.uscis.gov/citizenship/testupdates) before
an interview. This site is independent and is not affiliated with USCIS.
