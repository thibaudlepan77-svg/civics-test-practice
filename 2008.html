The 100 civics questions, the 2008 test

If you filed Form N-400 before October 20, 2025 this is your test. The officer asks up to 10 of these 100 questions and you need 6 correct. Filed on or after that date? You take the 128 question test instead, and this page explains which is which.

If you are 65 or older

With 20 years or more as a permanent resident you study only the 20 starred questions. The officer asks 10 of them and you need 6 correct, in the language of your choice.
