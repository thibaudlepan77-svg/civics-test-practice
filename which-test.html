Which test do I take, 100 questions or 128?

One date decides it, and it is the date you filed Form N-400, not your interview date.

You filed Form N-400Your test
Before October 20, 2025The 2008 test. 100 questions, the officer asks up to 10, you need 6 correct. Study it here.
On or after October 20, 2025The 2025 test. 128 questions, the officer asks up to 20, you need 12 correct, and the officer stops the moment you reach 12 or 9 wrong. Study it here.

The three things people get wrong

It is the filing date, not the interview date. An interview in 2026 on a form filed in early 2025 is still the 100 question test.

The 65/20 exemption exists in both. If you are 65 or older and have been a permanent resident for 20 years or more you study only the 20 starred questions, and you may answer in the language of your choice.

Four answers are never in the booklet. Your senators, your representative, your governor and your state capital change with where you live, so the official list says Answers will vary. They are filled in per state on this site, for both tests.
